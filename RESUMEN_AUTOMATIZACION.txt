TICKET 9477 — Guía de Remisión Manual Issues

Problem: When generating a Shipment Guide (Guía de Remisión) PDF in Openbravo,
         three fields remain empty/incorrect:
         1. Placa (License Plate) — not auto-filled from transportist
         2. Destinatario RUC/Company Name — missing expected values
         3. Agency address & phone — not shown in document footer

Cause: Jasper template used to generate the PDF doesn't have field mappings
       configured for transportist data (Placa), destination info (RUC, name),
       or agency location data.

Solution (Phase 1): Verify/configure Jasper template to include these fields
Solution (Phase 2): Check if Unnoparts custom triggers override template mapping

Workaround: Manual data entry in affected fields before generating PDF
Impact: Medium (affects commercial qualification with OEA Honda)
Risk: Configuration issue, not a system bug

TICKET 9478 — Pre-cancellation Credit Remaining After Payment

Problem: Customer CASTAÑEDA HERRERA requested pre-cancellation of full credit
         ($1,959.63). System generated:
         ✅ Credit Note 016-001-00130
         ✅ Payment 016-C1-10021552*Z*
         ❌ BUT customer credit still shows outstanding debt

Cause: The pre-cancellation process in Openbravo partially executed:
       - Generated the Credit Note (NC) ✅
       - Generated the Payment (Cobro) ✅
       - Did NOT apply/match the payment to customer invoices ❌
       
       Root cause: Missing or incomplete payment matching logic in the
       pre-cancellation trigger/process.

Immediate Workaround: Manually apply the payment to the customer's pending
                      invoices in the Payment application window.

Permanent Solution: Fix pre-cancellation process to execute payment matching
                   automatically after creating NC and Payment.

Impact: High (customer credit liability misreported, affects collections)
Risk: Process issue requiring development to fix permanently
